# ballast.

Your personal portfolio agent. Tell it what changed in your life — "I got laid off", "we're expecting twins", "my mom needs home care", "got a raise" — and it reviews your accounts and recommends what to do with your portfolio, with a reason for every trade. It never trades on its own: your advisor approves.

Under the hood: Muse (Meta) runs your agent on your connected data; every recommendation is checked against fixed client-protection rules, and the agent's skills improve over time only when the improvement is proven on clients it has never seen.

Demo profiles: Dana (rates trader), Miguel (ICU nurse), Aisha (product manager), Tom (engineer), Priya (designer). Say your first name, then what's going on.

Synthetic demo clients. Illustrative policy, not financial advice.
